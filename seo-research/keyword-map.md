2. **Homepage localization** — Cluster 1 primary kw + Cluster 6 messaging + LocalBusiness schema.
3. **NEW `/web-design-kitchener-waterloo`** — Cluster 1 + 5, near-me semantics, local case
   studies, custom-vs-template section, FAQ (from PAA/question data).
